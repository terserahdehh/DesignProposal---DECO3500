# Research Findings

This directory brings together the primary and secondary research for the
DECO3500 concert and music-festival experience project. All interview transcripts
are collected in one PDF; supporting research and evaluations are grouped by contributor.

## Research question

> How might technology help concert and festival attendees stay connected,
> communicate, and navigate crowded venues without distracting them from the
> live experience?

## Shared themes

- Reliable friend-finding matters when mobile reception is weak.
- Loud music and dense crowds make verbal coordination difficult.
- A wearable should reduce phone dependence without demanding constant attention.
- Accessibility features should be optional, adjustable, and designed for different needs.
- Simple venue cues and physical signage can be more useful than a complicated map.

## Research ethics and handling

The material in this directory is intended for the DECO3500 university project.
Participants should remain de-identified in public-facing outputs unless they
have explicitly consented to being named. Recording links and raw documents may
contain sensitive research data and should only be shared with the project team
and teaching staff as appropriate.
