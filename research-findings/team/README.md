## Google Forms survey evidence

![Original Google Forms screenshot showing 27 responses, with 48.1% selecting locating or reuniting with friends and 29.6% selecting communicating with friends, followed by visible written responses](images/google-forms-survey-evidence.png)

The screenshot records **27 responses** to a question about the greatest difficulty experienced at a concert or music festival. The two labelled percentages are **48.1%** for locating or reuniting with friends and **29.6%** for communicating with friends. Visible written responses describe poor reception, difficulty finding friends, loud music and crowded venues.

The supplied screenshot is preserved unchanged. Only percentages explicitly labelled in the image are reported here. This is contextual survey evidence, separate from the prototype usability-testing video.
